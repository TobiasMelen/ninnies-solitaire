idle.webp: AI-generated for Ninnies Solitaire with Nano Banana 2.1 via Weave (Figma), from a text
prompt describing the family's chocolate-and-white Holland Lop. Background removed locally (canvas
flood-fill of the plain studio backdrop), cropped and downscaled to 360x474 WebP.

happy.webp, sad.webp, thinking.webp, angry.webp: AI-generated sprite sheet supplied by the project
owner (2026-11-16). Split into poses locally, edges eroded 3px and de-fringed, all scaled by one
shared factor and bottom-aligned in the same 360x474 box as idle.

More poses (alert, aside, shock, love) are picked up automatically when added here as <pose>.webp
at the same size; until then the bunny falls back to the nearest pose it has.
