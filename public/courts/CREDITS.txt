Court card figures

Each set is cut out from vector originals for this game: only the figure artwork is kept
(no suit symbols, indices, frame or card background), areas enclosed by the figure are filled
white, and the result is exported as transparent WebP.

knoll/    "Classic" — Byron Knoll, "Vector Playing Cards". Public domain.
          https://code.google.com/archive/p/vector-playing-cards/
          via https://github.com/hayeah/playing-cards-assets

kennard/  "English" — Adrian Kennard (RevK), traced from a 19th-century Goodall & Son deck. CC0.
          https://www.me.uk/cards/

atlas/    "Atlas" — Dmitry Fomin, Russian "Atlas" pattern. CC0.
          https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Atlas_deck_king_of_spades.svg (and siblings)
